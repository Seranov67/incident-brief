# Incident Brief — working technical label
A browser-local way for an on-call administrator to turn a pasted application log into an evidence-backed handoff.
## The Unique Kernel
Group repeated failures while keeping traceable source-line numbers and redacting common secrets and addresses in every report. No upload or AI inference service.
## Who It's For
A solo system administrator preparing a concise incident handoff from a small log excerpt.
## The Core Loop
Open the tool, load the synthetic example or paste logs, analyze, inspect grouped findings and their evidence, download Markdown.
## Inspiration & Identity
Agent-selected operational notebook: warm paper, charcoal typography, restrained orange, readable monospace logs. No external fonts/assets.
## Why This Matters to the Learner
The user is a system administrator and delegated a small useful project. No invented personal story.
## What "Working" Looks Like
The bundled six-line example yields two errors and one warning, groups a repeated timeout, shows redacted evidence, and exports the same findings.
## The POC Boundary
One screen, pasted plain-text or JSONL logs up to 200 KiB/2000 lines, deterministic grouping, evidence and Markdown export.
## Later
Additional log formats and user-defined patterns.
## Explicitly Cut
Runtime LLM/API calls, production connections, automatic remediation, accounts and persistent storage: unnecessary for proving this loop in 8 hours.
